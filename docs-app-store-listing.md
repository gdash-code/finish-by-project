## Description (4000 characters max)

**For people who buy books but don't finish them.**

You love reading. You buy books with the best intentions. But life happens—work gets busy, weekends fill up, and that book on your nightstand stays at page 47 for months.

Finish By is different.

**No Guilt. No Streaks. Just Smart Pacing.**

Instead of shaming you for "breaking your streak," Finish By quietly recalculates your reading pace when you miss a day. You're never behind—you're just on a new timeline.

**Choose Your Commitment, Not Your Identity**

Pick how you want to read:
• **Gentle** - 4 days a week, maximum flexibility
• **Balanced** - 6 days a week, steady progress  
• **Intense** - Daily reading, build the habit

Your commitment level gives you built-in buffer days. Miss a few days? That's what the buffer is for.

**Adaptive Pacing That Respects Your Life**

Enter your book details and finish date. Finish By calculates your daily page target based on your commitment level and reading speed. As you update your progress, it automatically adjusts—no complicated math, no guilt.

**Messages That Actually Help**

Instead of:
❌ "You failed today"
❌ "Streak broken!"
❌ "You're 5 days behind"

You get:
✅ "Life happens. We've recalculated—15 pages/day gets you on track."
✅ "Halfway there! 12 days left."
✅ "You finished! Completed in 23 days 🎉"

**Simple Features, Big Impact**

• Set reading goals with flexible finish dates
• Daily page targets that adapt to your pace
• Buffer day tracking (your built-in skip days)
• Optional reminders on your reading days
• Progress visualization without pressure
• Suggested reading windows based on your pace

**Why Finish By Works:**

1. **Lifestyle First** - Commitment levels respect your real schedule
2. **Adaptive Math** - Automatically recalculates when you miss days
3. **No Gamification** - No streaks, badges, or leaderboards to maintain
4. **Transparent Difficulty** - See when your pace is getting unrealistic
5. **Always Escapable** - Extend your date anytime, no judgment

**Who Is This For?**

• You have unread books on your shelf (or Kindle)
• You set reading goals but don't finish them
• You hate apps that make you feel bad
• You want accountability without anxiety
• You read in bursts, not every single day

**Start Your Next Book**

No more guilt about unfinished books. No more broken streaks. Just you, a book, and a plan that adapts to your life.

Download Finish By and actually finish what you start.
